Nocturne, or Nox for short, is Starbie's cuddly companion! He's a black wyvern with rainbow patterning and a star-shaped mark on his forehead. Nox is roughly the size of a large cat and he loves pets. Allegedly, he came from a shooting star, or rather, he WAS a shooting star himself. The star at the end of his tail (not pictured) shines brighter the happier he is!
