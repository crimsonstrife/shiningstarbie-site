Born as Astaria, the Succuba of Starlight, I was regarded with admiration and respect among the ranks of the Celestial Circle. I was an expertly trained assassin tasked with claiming the souls of the world's most rotten deviants, like murderers and abusers. I was pretty damn good at it too.

Or at least, that's what I had been led to believe... I should have known better, that it was all too good to be true. If only I had seen through their lies sooner, maybe...just maybe, I could have saved her.

For now, I'm on the run from my past. I want to learn what it means to be human. For her. As evil and wicked as humanity can be, I hope I can understand just how beautiful it is too. If I can be half the person she was, that'd make me very happy.

Don't think I won't get my revenge though... I still know my way around a knife.
